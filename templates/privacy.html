Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

The short version

  • We collect only what Spendly needs to work: your account and your expenses.
  • We never sell your data or show you ads.
  • Passwords are hashed, and your expenses are visible only to you.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time.

This policy explains what information Spendly collects, why we collect it, and what control you have over it.

1What We Collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and a hashed version of your password.
  • Expense records: the amounts, categories, dates and descriptions you enter.
  • Session data: a cookie that keeps you signed in while you use the app.

We don't collect bank details, card numbers or your location.

2How We Use It

We use your information only to:

  • Create your account and sign you in.
  • Store your expenses and show you summaries and breakdowns.
  • Keep the service secure and fix problems.

3Storage and Security

Your data is stored in Spendly's database. Passwords are hashed before they're saved, so not even we can read them. Each account can see only its own expenses.

No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access.

4Sharing

We don't sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only if the law requires us to.

5Your Rights

  • See and edit the expenses you've entered at any time.
  • Ask for a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Delete your account, along with all of its expense records.

6Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Using Spendly after a change means you accept the updated policy. See also our Terms and Conditions.

7Contact

Questions about your privacy? Reach us at support@spendly.com.
